    How it works

    1. Each buyer sends exactly 0.001 ETH per coupon. The 22-day clock starts at the first purchase.
    2. On the 10th coupon, sales close and the contract asks Chainlink VRF for a random number.
    3. The winner is coupon number (random mod 10). Winners pull their prize with Withdraw.
    4. If 22 days pass with fewer than 10 coupons, the round becomes refundable.
